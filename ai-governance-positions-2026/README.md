# AI Governance Positions, 2026

A source-led Omoluabi comparison of major corporate, governmental and multilateral positions on frontier AI risk and governance.

## Outputs

- `index.html` — public report, position matrix and evidence map.
- `SOURCE_NOTES.md` — source ledger.
- `data/positions.json` — structured actor-position records.
- `data/evidence-map.json` — actor-to-governance-mechanism evidence graph.

## Method

No actor is ranked, scored or declared the winner of a policy debate. The visualization encodes documented public positions across governance mechanisms and preserves contradictions within an actor's own position when sources support them.
