## Editorial rule
A public statement about AI risk does not establish motive. Corporate incentives, national strategy, safety concerns, market competition and public-interest commitments can coexist. Omoluabi records the stated position and the evidence supporting it; claims about hidden motives remain hypotheses unless separately evidenced.
