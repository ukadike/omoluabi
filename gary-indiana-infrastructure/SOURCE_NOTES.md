## 2. City of Gary — Storm Recovery & Support

**Source:** City of Gary storm-recovery resource page  
**URL:** https://www.gary.gov/stormrecovery

### Supports
- YWCA at 150 W. 15th Ave. was listed as a 24/7 shelter and charging station.
- The city opened a medical oxygen refill station at Indiana University Northwest for home-use patients affected by the August 11 storms/outages.
- Food and other emergency resources were distributed during recovery.

### Why this matters
These response resources demonstrate that the outage affected more than lighting. It created needs around charging, shelter, oxygen, food, and medically essential home equipment.

### Does not establish
- The total number of residents needing each service.
- Whether every listed site had independent generator power.

## 3. City of Gary — AT&T / connectivity recovery update

**Source:** City of Gary, August 18, 2026  
**URL:** https://www.gary.gov/news-updates/mayor-melton-provides-storm-recovery-updates-with-congressman-mrvan-and-announces-partnership-with-at-t

### Supports
- AT&T provided charging lockers at Red Cross-supported shelters including the Gary YWCA.
- Verizon provided Wi-Fi and charging at the YWCA.
- FirstNet deployed a satellite truck to the NIPSCO facility in Gary to support utility recovery efforts.
- By August 18 the city had assessed 2,097 damage sites/assistance requests; more than 80% involved fallen trees and blocked roads, and 75% of reported blocked roads had reopened.

### Why this matters
A satellite deployment to support utility recovery shows that **communications continuity is itself part of electric restoration infrastructure**. Charging/Wi-Fi deployments also show resident connectivity was a recovery need.

### Does not establish
- Citywide cellular outage magnitude.
- Whether 911/CAD/public-safety radio systems failed.
- Whether FirstNet was replacing failed service or adding capacity/redundancy.

## 7. CBS Chicago — schools, fuel pumps, prolonged outage

**Source:** CBS Chicago, August 19, 2026  
**URL:** https://www.cbsnews.com/chicago/news/buildings-homes-still-without-power-northwest-indiana/

### Supports — credible reporting
- Gary public schools remained closed because of ongoing storm-related issues.
- More than 27,000 Gary buildings/homes were reported without power eight days after the storm.
- A Citgo station in Gary had nonfunctioning gas pumps; a generator kept part of the business operating, but fuel for that generator had to be obtained elsewhere.

### Why this matters
This illustrates a dependency loop: **electricity → fuel pump operation → generator/vehicle fuel → recovery capacity**.

# Highest-value unresolved questions

1. **Electricity:** What was the exact restoration order by feeder/circuit/neighborhood and critical facility?
2. **Traffic:** Which signals failed and when were they restored?
3. **Roads:** Which neighborhoods lost practical access because of blocked routes?
4. **Fuel:** How many stations could not pump fuel, and how did this affect generators, residents, transit, and emergency fleets?
5. **Communications:** What cellular degradation occurred, and why was satellite/temporary connectivity deployed?
6. **911/public safety:** Did CAD, 911, radio, fire/police facilities, or dispatch experience failover or degraded service?
7. **Water:** Were the August main breaks related to storm damage, pressure changes, power loss, age, or unrelated failures?
8. **Wastewater:** Did treatment/pumping experience power loss, generator operation, overflow, or bypass?
9. **Medical:** How did Northlake and home medical-device users maintain continuity?
10. **Transit/accessibility:** Did fixed route, paratransit, wheelchair transport, or access to shelters/medical services degrade?
11. **Schools:** Which facilities lacked power/access and what determined reopening?
12. **Surveillance:** Did RTCC/Flock/other sensors remain operational, and through what redundancy?

# Do not collapse these claims

```text
Gary suffered a prolonged large-scale electric outage.                       SUPPORTED
Traffic-signal and fuel-access failures occurred in Gary.                    REPORTED / STRONGLY SUPPORTED
Emergency charging, connectivity, shelter, and oxygen support were deployed. CONFIRMED
Water-main breaks/boil advisories occurred during recovery.                  CONFIRMED EVENT
The storm caused those water-main breaks.                                    NOT ESTABLISHED
Wastewater treatment failed.                                                 NOT ESTABLISHED
Gary's hospital lost power or services.                                      NOT ESTABLISHED
911/dispatch failed.                                                         NOT ESTABLISHED
Gary transit service failed.                                                 NOT ESTABLISHED
Gary operates RTCC/LPR and has documented Flock deployment.                  CONFIRMED
Gary's Flock cameras stayed online during the blackout.                      NOT ESTABLISHED
Gary prioritized surveillance over residents.                               NOT ESTABLISHED
```

That separation is the Omoluabi method.
