# Reporter Records Guide: Iron Nation–Indiana

This page is a document-acquisition guide for reporters and public-interest researchers. It separates records already public from records that should be requested or located.

## Priority documents to seek

### 1. Executed instrument governing Indiana's $15 million

**Ask IEDC / Office of Commerce for:**

- executed investment agreement;
- subscription agreement;
- limited-partner agreement or joinder;
- side letter;
- memorandum of understanding;
- award agreement;
- incentive agreement;
- tax-credit agreement;
- grant agreement;
- amendments, schedules, exhibits and term sheets.

**Why it matters:** This is the document most likely to establish whether the state's commitment is a fund investment, incentive package, tax-credit structure, mixed structure, or another vehicle.

### 2. March 18, 2026 IEDC board approval record

**Ask for:**

- full agenda and agenda packet;
- staff memorandum;
- board resolution;
- roll-call or vote record;
- Investment Capital Program approval materials;
- attachments not included in the public recap;
- minutes, draft minutes and final minutes;
- any executive-session notice or certification that legally may be disclosed.

**Search point:** The April 16 State Budget Committee discussion references a $15 million approval associated with the March 18 IEDC meeting. The public March 18 recap should not be treated as the complete board record.

### 3. Twenty-First Century Research and Technology Fund transaction file

**Ask for:**

- project or transaction identifier;
- funding authorization;
- source-of-funds memorandum;
- disbursement authorization;
- payment or transfer records;
- investment committee materials;
- program eligibility analysis;
- accounting classification;
- ledger entries showing obligation and disbursement dates.

### 4. Capital movement and return records

**Ask for:**

- capital-call notices;
- wire or transfer confirmations, with account numbers redacted where necessary;
- notices of admission as an investor;
- quarterly or annual investor statements;
- distributions;
- valuation statements;
- fee statements;
- management-fee provisions;
- carried-interest provisions;
- return or repayment schedule;
- audit reports relating to the investment.

**Why it matters:** These records can distinguish an announced commitment from money actually transferred and can establish whether taxpayer capital is pooled, segregated or attributable to named investments.

### 5. Portfolio attribution records

**Ask for:**

- portfolio-company list for the vehicle receiving Indiana capital;
- dates and amounts of each investment;
- follow-on investments;
- allocation methodology for pooled capital;
- any Indiana-specific sleeves, sidecars or special-purpose vehicles;
- reports identifying companies establishing Indiana operations.

**Do not infer:** A portfolio company's presence in Iron Nation materials does not establish that Indiana capital financed that company.

### 6. Defense and dual-use diligence

**Ask for records that identify:**

- defense, security or dual-use classifications used by IEDC or Iron Nation;
- diligence memoranda concerning export controls, sanctions, ITAR/EAR issues or restricted end uses;
- sector exclusions or investment restrictions;
- representations made to Indiana about military or government customers;
- any Indiana review of defense-sector portfolio exposure.

### 7. Intermediary and communications records

**Search names / entities:**

- Iron Nation;
- Iron Nation II, Limited Partnership;
- Iron Nation II G.P, Limited Partnership;
- GP Iron Nation 2 Ltd;
- Gil Friedlander;
- Chen Linchevski;
- Jason Wolf;
- Luke Messer;
- Prolific;
- Indiana Economic Development Corporation;
- Office of Commerce;
- Governor's Office.

**Ask for:** emails, attachments, calendar invitations, meeting notes, pitch decks, presentations, due-diligence materials, term sheets, correspondence and messaging records concerning the transaction.

Use a defined date range rather than asking for 'all records.' A useful first pass is **January 1, 2025 through April 30, 2026**, followed by narrower requests based on what the first production reveals.

### 8. Ethics, lobbying and conflicts

Seek:

- conflict-of-interest disclosures and recusals;
- ethics reviews;
- consultant contracts;
- intermediary compensation agreements;
- lobbying registrations or reports, where applicable;
- procurement determinations or sole-source justifications, if any;
- legal opinions about IEDC authority for the structure.

### 9. Performance obligations in Indiana

Seek the record establishing whether portfolio companies must:

- locate headquarters or operations in Indiana;
- create a minimum number of jobs;
- meet wage thresholds;
- make capital investments;
- partner with Indiana companies, universities or health systems;
- remain in Indiana for a defined period;
- repay or forfeit benefits if targets are missed.

## Suggested APRA wording

> Pursuant to Indiana's Access to Public Records Act, I request records sufficient to show the legal and financial structure of the State of Indiana's announced $15 million commitment to Iron Nation–Indiana, including the executed agreement or agreements governing the commitment, associated board approvals, staff memoranda, term sheets, amendments, funding authorizations, disbursement records and records identifying the recipient legal entity. Please produce reasonably segregable non-exempt portions of any responsive record and identify the statutory basis for each withholding or redaction.

Then make narrower follow-up requests for the specific record classes above.

## Reporting discipline

A document proving **Indiana → Iron Nation–Indiana** does not automatically prove **Indiana → Iron Nation II → portfolio company X**. Each legal and financial edge should have its own source.

When a requested document is obtained, preserve:

- source URL or agency;
- request date;
- production date;
- filename as received;
- page number supporting each claim;
- whether redactions exist;
- any agency explanation for withholding;
- a cryptographic hash if the newsroom's preservation workflow supports it.
