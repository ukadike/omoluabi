# Iron Nation–Indiana — Source Ledger

Last reviewed: 2026-08-31

This ledger is for reporters and researchers. Inclusion means a source is relevant to the investigation; it does not mean Omoluabi adopts every claim in that source.

## Tier 1 — Primary / official records

### Indiana Economic Development Corporation — April 13, 2026

**Title:** Governor Braun Announces Launch of Iron Nation–Indiana to Drive Innovation, Investment and Economic Growth

**URL:**  
https://iedc.in.gov/events/news/details/2026/04/13/governor-braun-announces-launch-of-iron-nation-indiana-to-drive-innovation-investment-and-economic-growth

**Establishes:**
- Indiana publicly announced Iron Nation–Indiana as a $60+ million investment and commercialization initiative.
- The state described the initiative as anchored by a $15 million investment from the State of Indiana.
- The announcement described more than $30 million as committed by the Iron Nation partnership.
- The initiative is intended to connect Indiana institutions and companies with Israeli technology companies.

**Does not establish:**
- the executed legal investment instrument;
- whether Indiana is directly an LP in Iron Nation II;
- capital-call mechanics or fund-flow attribution;
- that Indiana dollars reached any specific portfolio company.

### Indiana State Budget Committee — April 16, 2026 minutes

**URL:**  
https://www.in.gov/sba/files/April-2026-Minutes.pdf

**Relevant passage:** page 5 of the PDF.

**Establishes:**
- Senator Fady Qaddoura referred to a March 18 approval of a $15 million Innovation Capital Program investment and asked who the recipient was.
- He then asked whether the governor's announced $15 million Iron Nation investment was the same investment.
- IEDC President Josh Richardson said that was possible but he was not sure without more information in front of him.
- The minutes record questions about when taxpayers could expect a timeline for dollars to come back to the state.

**Why it matters:**
The official record itself preserves an unresolved connection between a prior $15 million approval and the Iron Nation announcement. Reporters should not silently resolve that ambiguity without the March 18 approval documents or executed transaction records.

## Omoluabi source rule

A source proving **A → B** and another proving **B → C** does not automatically prove **A's money → C**, especially inside a pooled investment vehicle. Fund-level association and portfolio-level attribution are separate evidentiary questions.
