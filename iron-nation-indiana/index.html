Iron Nation–Indiana

A living source dossier for journalists, researchers, and public-interest investigators.

What this resource is

This is not an article and it does not begin from a conclusion. It organizes primary records, credible reporting, unresolved claims, and reporting leads around Indiana's $15 million commitment to the Iron Nation–Indiana initiative and questions about Iron Nation II, intermediaries, and defense or dual-use investments.

Editorial rule: association is not attribution. A fund's sector activity does not by itself prove that Indiana's specific capital financed a particular company or use.

Current claim status

Confirmed

Indiana publicly announced a $15 million state investment anchoring Iron Nation–Indiana.

Confirmed

SEC Form D identifies Iron Nation II, Limited Partnership as an Israel-organized pooled investment / venture capital fund.

Reported

Indiana Capital Chronicle reports the $15 million comes from the Twenty-First Century Research Fund. The underlying transaction record remains a priority document.

Not yet documented

The executed record proving whether Indiana invested directly into Iron Nation II, through another vehicle, or through a mixed structure has not yet been located in this dossier.

Not established

Indiana's specific $15 million financed a named defense or dual-use portfolio company.

Not established

Indiana taxpayer money financed weapons production.
