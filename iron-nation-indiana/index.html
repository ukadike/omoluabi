Iron Nation–Indiana

A living source dossier and reporting toolkit for journalists, researchers, and public-interest investigators.

What this resource is

This is not an article and it does not begin from a conclusion. It organizes primary records, credible reporting, unresolved claims, missing documents, reporting routes, and relationship maps around Indiana's $15 million commitment to the Iron Nation–Indiana initiative and questions about Iron Nation II, intermediaries, and defense or dual-use investments.

Editorial rule: association is not attribution. A fund's sector activity does not by itself prove that Indiana's specific capital financed a particular company or use.

Central reporting gap

The most important missing record is the executed instrument governing Indiana's $15 million commitment. It should identify the recipient legal entity, the nature of the state's financial interest or incentive, performance conditions, rights to returns, reporting requirements, fees, restrictions, and—if applicable—how Indiana capital participates in a pooled investment vehicle.
