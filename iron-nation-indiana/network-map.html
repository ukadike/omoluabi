How to read this map

Omoluabi treats every relationship as a separate evidentiary edge. A source may establish one edge without establishing the next. Solid relationships are supported by records currently in the dossier. Dashed relationships are unresolved and must not be silently completed by inference.

  • Confirmed: primary or official record supports the edge.
  • Reported: credible reporting supports it; underlying record still sought.
  • Unresolved: decisive documentary link is missing.
  • Not established: current evidence does not prove the claim.

INI-001 · State of Indiana → $15M Iron Nation–Indiana commitment · CONFIRMED
What proves it
Indiana Economic Development Corporation's April 13, 2026 launch announcement states that the initiative is anchored by a $15 million investment from the State of Indiana.
Primary source
IEDC announcement — April 13, 2026
What it does not prove
The announcement does not by itself establish the recipient legal entity, executed terms, capital-call status, or portfolio-company attribution.
Reporter should seek
Executed agreement, funding authorization, payment/disbursement record, and recipient legal-entity documentation.
Request target
IEDC / Indiana Office of Commerce.
INI-002 · $15M commitment → Twenty-First Century Research Fund · REPORTED
What supports it
Indiana Capital Chronicle reported that the state's $15 million commitment comes from the Twenty-First Century Research Fund.
Reporting source
Indiana Capital Chronicle — April 13, 2026
What is missing
The underlying award, authorization, ledger, transfer, or investment record establishing the fund source.
Reporter should seek
Source-of-funds memorandum, transaction identifier, funding authorization, disbursement record and ledger entries.
Request target
IEDC / Office of Commerce and the custodian for Twenty-First Century Research Fund records.
INI-004 · Indiana $15M → Iron Nation II, Limited Partnership · UNRESOLVED
What is known
Indiana announced a $15 million Iron Nation–Indiana investment. Separately, an SEC Form D identifies Iron Nation II, Limited Partnership as an Israel-organized pooled investment / venture-capital fund.
Relevant sources
IEDC launch announcement · SEC Form D
Why the edge stays open
Two documents proving A→B and B-related facts do not establish Indiana's capital path into Iron Nation II. The executed investment instrument has not been located in this dossier.
Reporter should seek
LP subscription agreement or joinder, side letter, award/incentive agreement, memorandum of understanding, term sheet and amendments.
Request target
IEDC / Office of Commerce. Ask specifically for records sufficient to identify the recipient legal entity.
INI-005 · March 18, 2026 $15M approval → Iron Nation transaction · UNRESOLVED
What supports inquiry
April 16 State Budget Committee minutes reference a $15 million approval connected to the March 18 IEDC meeting; IEDC President Josh Richardson indicated the connection to Iron Nation was possible but said he was not sure without more information.
Official record
State Budget Committee minutes — April 16, 2026
Related public recap
IEDC March 18 board recap
Reporter should seek
Full March 18 agenda packet, staff memo, board resolution, vote record, Innovation Capital Program approval materials and attachments.
Request target
IEDC / Office of Commerce.
INI-007 · Indiana capital → named defense / dual-use portfolio company · NOT ESTABLISHED
What is documented
Defense and dual-use technology appears in the public initiative context, including the IEDC announcement's discussion of Indiana's defense ecosystem.
What is not documented
No record currently in this dossier attributes Indiana's specific $15 million to a named defense or dual-use portfolio company.
Reporter should seek
Portfolio-company investment schedule, capital-allocation methodology, sidecar/SPV records, investor reports, sector diligence and any Indiana-specific portfolio reporting.
Request target
IEDC / Office of Commerce for state-held records; fund-side records where independently obtainable.
INI-008 · Indiana taxpayer money → weapons production · NOT ESTABLISHED
Current evidence
The available record does not establish this proposition.
What would be required
A documented capital path from Indiana into a named company plus evidence of the relevant use of funds. Sector classification alone is insufficient.
Reporter should seek
Investment and use-of-proceeds records, relevant contracts, investor reporting and any restrictions or representations governing military/end-use exposure.
Editorial caution
Do not substitute association, sector proximity or pooled-fund membership for capital attribution.
