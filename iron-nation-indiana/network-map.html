How to read this map

Solid nodes represent entities or relationships supported by public records in this dossier. Dashed nodes represent missing legal or financial links that reporters should seek. The map does not imply that pooled capital can be attributed to a specific portfolio company without documentary support.

What would change this map?

Portfolio allocation records could establish whether attribution to a particular company is possible. Until then, those edges remain visibly unresolved.
