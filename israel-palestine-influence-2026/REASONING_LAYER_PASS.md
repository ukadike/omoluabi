# Omoluabi Reasoning Layer Pass — Israel–Palestine Aid and U.S. Political Influence

**Case:** Federal assistance, state tax-share attribution, lobbying disclosures, campaign finance, and 2026 candidate records.  
**Cutoff:** November 3, 2026. **Status:** Human-governed research interpretation.  
**Rule:** AI assists. Rules govern. Humans publish.

## Core finding
Federal records support national aid totals and disclosed lobbying/election-finance streams. They do not show states directly paying country-specific aid, a taxpayer-to-program trace, or state-priced lobbying totals. State amounts are modeled allocations; influence records describe distinct activities.

## 1. Narrative Probability
Known: federal obligations and disclosed political-finance records. Not established: state payments, taxpayer tracing, or spending causing votes. Possible: candidate races attract differing levels of support; each race needs source-level review.

## 2. Historical Erasure Detection
A single aid number erases obligations versus disbursements and military versus humanitarian/economic programs. A single lobby total erases contributions, independent expenditures, endorsements, and lobbying. Keep the source categories visible.

## 3. Source Ladder
Prioritize ForeignAssistance.gov, IRS, FEC, LDA.gov, CRS, and GAO. Label Pew's dated extraction and FundVoter's FEC-based candidate tracker as secondary. Cite advocacy groups only for their own endorsements.

## 4. Contradiction
Preserve Israel FY2025 obligations ($3.3048B), the July 2026 disbursement snapshot ($3.3107B), and the CRS broader figure ($3.8B) as different scopes/dates. West Bank/Gaza obligations total $565.3M with a different program mix.

## 5. Timeline Integrity
Separate fiscal year, tax year, lobbying quarter, election cycle, filing date, and tracker refresh date. Do not substitute a publication date for an event or accounting period.

## 6. Returnability Index
Keep source URLs, CSV rows, candidate roster links, and dated snapshots available. Corrections should retain old values, new values, reason, and date.

## 7. Dangerous Words
Say modeled allocation, not state gave. Say lobbying disclosure, FEC contribution, independent expenditure, or endorsement only for that record type. Avoid bought/controls/caused without evidence.

## 8. Local Testimony
No state residents or aid recipients were interviewed. Federal records do not represent lived experience.

## 9. Map–Data Integrity
Use the same state fields in map, table, and CSV; display source and methodology; include a text table. Geographic scope is 50 states plus DC. Candidate money is mapped by candidate state, not donor residence.

## 10. Ceasefire Distortion
This dataset does not measure ceasefire compliance, civilian harm, or conflict outcomes. Do not infer them from aid or political finance.

## 11. Survival-Systems
Separate Israel military/security assistance from West Bank/Gaza economic/humanitarian obligations. Do not infer program purpose from a state allocation.

## 12. Education Continuity
No school-level or education-continuity outcomes are measured. Do not claim local education impact from these totals.

## 13. Legal / Ethical Caution
Disclosures do not prove wrongdoing, coordination, motive, or policy influence. Do not infer identity or allegiance from advocacy.

## 14. Unrecoverable Loss Register
Potential gaps: pending or amended filings; incomplete donor attribution; no taxpayer-to-program trace; no state allocation for lobbying; no complete movement-wide Palestinian-rights spending total. No loss of source files is identified.

## 15. Temporal Justice
Preserve dated snapshots and versioned methods. Future editors should reproduce what readers saw at this cutoff and correct the record without erasing prior evidence.
