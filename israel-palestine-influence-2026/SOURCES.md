## State allocation
Method A: national FY2025 obligations multiplied by each state/DC share of IRS FY2025 gross collections from [IRS Data Book Table 1-5](https://www.irs.gov/statistics/soi-tax-stats-gross-collections-by-type-of-tax-and-state-irs-data-book-table-1-5). Method B: same obligation total multiplied by normalized state/DC share of TY2023 IRS individual income-tax amounts after credits from [IRS SOI Historic Table 2](https://www.irs.gov/statistics/soi-tax-stats-historic-table-2). The denominator is 50 states plus DC. Neither method traces a particular taxpayer's dollars into aid; IRS filings and address conventions have limits. These are estimates, not state payments.

## Measurement rules
Obligations, disbursements, CRS program totals, lobbying expenses, candidate contributions, independent expenditures, and endorsements answer different questions. Reporting periods are not merged. Amounts alone do not establish motive, wrongdoing, or causation. See the accompanying CSVs for full state rows.
