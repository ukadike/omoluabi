Omoluabi Reasoning Layer v2

Fifteen-layer research pass

  1. Narrative probability: national records support aid and disclosure totals; no state payment or donor-causation claim.
  2. Historical erasure: preserve aid program mix and obligations/disbursement distinctions.
  3. Source ladder: prioritize agency, IRS, FEC, LDA, CRS, and GAO records; label secondary trackers.
  4. Contradiction: keep the $3.305B, $3.311B, and $3.8B Israel totals separate by scope/date.
  5. Timeline integrity: distinguish fiscal year, tax year, filing quarter, election cycle, and retrieval date.
  6. Returnability: retain URLs, source registry, and downloadable raw state tables.
  7. Dangerous words: say modeled allocation, not state gave.
  8. Local testimony: no residents or aid recipients were interviewed in this record-based POC.
  9. Map–data integrity: show denominator, field, provenance, caveat, and accessible state table.
  10. Ceasefire distortion: these records do not measure ceasefire or infer policy causation.
  11. Survival systems: distinguish military/security assistance from economic/humanitarian obligations.
  12. Education continuity: no school-level outcome is measured or inferred.
  13. Legal/ethical caution: disclose activity; do not infer wrongdoing, motive, or influence.
  14. Unrecoverable loss: donor attribution, pending filings, and taxpayer-to-program trace remain incomplete.
  15. Temporal justice: preserve dated snapshots and correction history rather than overwrite.

Source ladder and method

Return to the records

Annotated source register • CSVs in data/. Primary records: ForeignAssistance.gov Israel, ForeignAssistance.gov West Bank and Gaza, IRS gross collections, IRS SOI tables, FEC AIPAC PAC, FEC UDP, and LDA.gov.

Allocation method

Method A multiplies the national FY2025 obligation total by each state’s normalized share of IRS FY2025 gross collections. Method B uses the state's normalized share of IRS TY2023 individual income tax after credits. Neither traces tax dollars or represents a state payment.

Measure differences

Obligations, disbursements, CRS program totals, lobbying expenses, candidate contributions, independent expenditures, and endorsements answer different questions and are never combined as a single total.

Candidate map limitation

AIPAC-linked state values aggregate candidate profiles on a 2026 federal roster from 2022–26, not 2026-only gifts. Lobbying is not allocated to a state. Outside election spending is distinct from candidate contributions.
