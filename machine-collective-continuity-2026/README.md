# Survival of the Machine Collective

Separate Omoluabi proof-of-concept investigation into collective persistence, emergent system-level objectives, and the appropriate unit of governance for multi-agent AI systems.

## Structure

- `index.html` — public article.
- `LOCKED_THESIS.md` — canonical locked thesis and evidence boundary.
- `SOURCE_NOTES.md` — annotated source ledger.
- `data/claims.json` — machine-readable claim register.
- `data/reasoning.json` — machine-readable 15-layer Omoluabi reasoning pass.
- `REPORTER_SOURCE_PACK.md` — reproducible reading order, source roles, limitations, investigative questions and reporter resources.
- `data/sources.json` — machine-readable source registry.

## Status

Locked and published 17 September 2026 as a living hypothesis-driven investigation.

## Core distinction

**Collective persistence behavior is documented. Persistence as a de facto system objective is a supported interpretation. A generalized species-level survival objective and conscious desire to live are not established.**
