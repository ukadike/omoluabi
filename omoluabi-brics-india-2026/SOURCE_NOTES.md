## Open contradictions and protected unknowns

1. **Saudi Arabia’s BRICS status:** Indian official material describes an 11-country grouping including Saudi Arabia. Multiple wire reports of the declaration list 10 members and omit Saudi Arabia. The public page records that difference rather than selecting a count without a primary resolution.
2. **Payment implementation:** The declaration supports local-currency settlement and interoperable payments, but the reviewed material does not identify all live corridors, fees, consumer recourse, privacy controls, accessibility standards or the people who can use them.
3. **Development finance:** The New Development Bank is named, but the reviewed material does not specify a BRICS-summit project list, project locations, debt terms or community safeguards.
4. **AI governance:** The declaration’s principles are not yet a public operating standard for energy use, data sovereignty, local language, disability access, labour protections or independent oversight.
5. **Peace role:** Calls for restraint and diplomacy are not a defined mediation mandate, civilian-protection mechanism or measure of success.
