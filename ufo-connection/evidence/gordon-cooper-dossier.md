**Status:** Living investigation

**Editorial rule:** Observation, archival fact, official interpretation, later recollection, and hypothesis remain separate.

## Why Cooper belongs in the archive

L. Gordon Cooper Jr. was a U.S. Air Force test pilot, one of NASA's original Mercury Seven, pilot of Mercury-Atlas 9 (Faith 7), and command pilot of Gemini 5. NASA records that he trained and worked at Edwards Air Force Base before joining the astronaut corps.

Official NASA biography: https://www.nasa.gov/history/mercury-seven-astronaut-biographies/

Cooper matters here because his public UFO record spans multiple evidence types: later recollections of a 1951 European observation; the documented 1957 Edwards photographic case; a 1962 television interview; real-time 1963 Mercury audio; a 1978 letter to the UN mission of Grenada; and much later interviews that strengthened the Edwards story into a landing claim.

## Source ladder

### A. Contemporaneous / institutional records

1. **NARA film inventory — Edwards AFB, Gettys & Bittick**  
   The National Archives Project Blue Book film inventory lists item **1003460**, dated **May 2, 1957**, Edwards AFB, Gettys & Bittick, described as **two 35mm black-and-white negative filmstrips**.  
   Source: https://prologue.blogs.archives.gov/wp-content/uploads/sites/9/2018/03/report.pdf

2. **1962 Cronkite–Cooper interview**  
   NARA lists *Walter Cronkite and Gordon Cooper on UFOs* as **National Archives Identifier 86027191**. In 2026, AARO/DVIDS released the excerpt as **NASA-UAP-D023**.  
   NARA: https://www.archives.gov/research/topics/uaps/moving-images-and-sound  
   DVIDS: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/1010337/nasa-uap-d023-interview-excerpt-with-astronaut-gordon-cooper-1962

3. **Mercury-Atlas 9 mission audio**  
   NASA/AARO released two Cooper excerpts in 2026. D010 records the familiar "fireflies" sequence and its condensation explanation. D011 records sunrise, luminous particles, and mission-related strobe-beacon context.  
   D010: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/1007874/nasa-uap-d010-mercury-atlas-9-audio-excerpt-may-15-1963  
   D011: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/1007876/nasa-uap-d011-mercury-atlas-9-audio-excerpt-may-15-1963

4. **1968 House UFO symposium**  
   Physicist James E. McDonald told the House Committee on Science and Astronautics that he had interviewed Edwards camera operators James D. Bittick and John R. Gettys Jr. and supervisor Frank E. Baker. McDonald described their account of photographing a domed-disc-like object that moved away into the distance. His reconstruction does **not** describe landing legs or a touchdown.  
   Official hearing record: https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/CHRG-90hhrg97818  
   Searchable reproduction of McDonald's statement: https://files.ncas.org/ufosymposium/mcdonald.html

### B. Later first-person statements

5. **Cooper's 1978 letter**  
   Cooper wrote to Ambassador Griffith of Grenada's UN mission advocating a coordinated scientific program for collecting and analyzing UFO reports. He also stated his personal belief that extraterrestrial vehicles were visiting Earth and referred to a 1951 European observation. The letter is evidence of **Cooper's belief and recollection**, not independent proof of the origin of the reported objects.  
   Archival scan location: https://s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/NARAprodstorage/lz/presidential-libraries/clinton/7262140/Batch0001/7262140-20060474F-001-010-2023.pdf

6. **Later Edwards landing account**  
   In later interviews Cooper described the Edwards event as a craft extending landing gear, touching down, being approached by the camera crew, and departing. This is materially stronger than the account McDonald attributed to Bittick and Gettys in 1968. Omoluabi therefore stores it as a separate testimony layer rather than silently merging the two narratives.

## Current finding

The strongest documentary fact is not "alien landing." It is this:

**A photographed 1957 Edwards AFB event has an identifiable archival trail, named professional range-camera witnesses, surviving cataloged film material, an official Air Force investigation, and a later congressional reconstruction. Gordon Cooper's later landing story is connected to this case but is not yet corroborated by the earlier witness reconstruction.**

That makes it testable.
