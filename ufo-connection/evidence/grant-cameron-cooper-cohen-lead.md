# Grant Cameron Lead — Gordon Cooper, William Cohen, and the Edwards Film

**Status:** Evidence lead with partial independent corroboration  
**Updated:** 24 September 2026  
**Editorial rule:** Cameron's reporting, Greer's retelling, archival FOIA outcomes, and Christopher Mellon's first-person account remain distinct.

## Why this lead matters

Grant Cameron was pursuing the Gordon Cooper / Edwards AFB film story by 2001. His significance to the Omoluabi investigation is not that he proves the landing story, but that he identified a specific **Cooper → Secretary of Defense William Cohen → government search for the film** chain years before Christopher Mellon publicly described being tasked by Cohen's office to investigate Cooper's claim.

## 1. Cameron's 2001 report

On November 27, 2001, Grant Cameron posted a message to the UFO UpDates list titled **"Gordon Cooper Talks UFOs With Defense Secretary."**

Cameron relayed an August 30, 2001 statement by Steven Greer about a "very famous astronaut" who had given Secretary of Defense William Cohen information about a specific piece of UFO evidence. Cameron identified that astronaut as Gordon Cooper and said the matter involved a UFO landing film.

Preserved reproduction:
https://www.jamesoberg.com/effort_locate_cooper_ufo_report.pdf

### Evidence status

- **Cameron statement:** documented.
- **Source Cameron relied on:** Steven Greer.
- **Cameron personally witnessed Cooper–Cohen contact:** not established.
- **At this stage:** hearsay chain, useful as an early dated lead.

## 2. Cameron turned the claim into targeted FOIA requests

On February 23, 2006, Cameron filed a set of Clinton Presidential Library FOIA requests. Two are directly relevant.

### FOIA 2006-0520-F

Request:

> All correspondence or records of meetings between Secretary of Defense William Cohen and Astronaut Gordon Cooper.

A NARA FOIA administration log records the request as completed March 14, 2006 with **no records provided**.

Source:
https://www.governmentattic.org/5docs/ChmIssaNARA_2011.pdf

### FOIA 2006-0521-F

Request:

> All files related to UFOs, the 1947 crash in Roswell, New Mexico, or flying saucers from the files of Secretary of Defense William Cohen.

This request did produce records. The Clinton Library finding aid says the responsive set is about **21 pages**. It found no relevant material in WHORM Alpha Files, WHORM Subject Files, or White House Staff and Office Files. The responsive material came from **NSC Cables** and consists of two documents sent by Cohen involving Department of Defense press briefings where UFOs/flying saucers were mentioned.

Official Clinton Library finding aid:
https://www.clintonlibrary.gov/research/archives/finding-aids/unidentified-flying-objects-ufo-2

### Omoluabi interpretation

The 0520 result does **not** prove Cooper never spoke to Cohen. It shows that Cameron's request did not produce responsive records from the searched Clinton Library holdings.

The 0521 result demonstrates that Cameron was systematically testing the Cohen connection through archives rather than merely repeating the story.

## 3. Christopher Mellon later provides direct corroboration of the government-search part

This is the strongest development.

Christopher Mellon, who served under Secretary Cohen at the Department of Defense, later said publicly that Cooper's claim was routed into Cohen's office and that **Cohen's office asked Mellon to pursue it**.

In the 2020 documentary *The Phenomenon*, Mellon says Cooper had spoken with the President, raised the matter with Secretary Cohen, and Cohen's office then called Mellon and asked him to "pursue this and chase it down." Mellon says an Air Force colonel eventually told him the records had been "cleaned up or thrown out to save space," wording Mellon himself described as sounding implausible.

Transcript source:
https://www.subtitlecat.com/subs/234/The.Phenomenon.2020.1080p.WEBRip.x264-RARBG-English.html

Mellon repeated the basic episode in a 2021 Joe Rogan interview. He said a request came to him—he thought from President Clinton—concerning an astronaut's claim that a UFO at Edwards had been videotaped and that the astronaut wanted the President to obtain the tape.

Transcript:
https://fight.fudgie.org/search/show/jr/episode/20210505_Wed_h8vjx6-ZFvI

### Why this materially changes the Cameron lead

Cameron in 2001:
**Cooper → Cohen → attempted retrieval of UFO landing film**

Mellon years later, speaking about his own government role:
**Cooper/President → Cohen's office → Mellon tasked to chase the records**

Those are not identical accounts, but they overlap on the central government-search chain.

This elevates Cameron's 2001 item from an isolated ufology rumor into an **early report later partially corroborated by a named former Defense official who says he personally received the tasking**.

It still does not corroborate:
- that a craft landed;
- that the film showed landing gear;
- that the film was hidden in a black program;
- that the records were deliberately destroyed;
- extraterrestrial origin.

## 4. Greer continued to make the same claim

In 2006, Greer again said Cooper personally told him he had spoken to then-Secretary Cohen about the Edwards footage and that Cohen's inquiry did not locate it.

Summary:

In a 2013 Art Bell appearance, Greer again described the Cooper–Cohen search and interpreted the failure to retrieve the film as evidence it was held in a black project. That final interpretation is Greer's, not established by Mellon or the archival record.

## Current conclusion

Grant Cameron is important because his 2001 report appears to preserve an early version of a **government retrieval effort** that Christopher Mellon later described from inside the Defense Department. Cameron's archival work also provides a concrete paper trail showing that he tried to verify the Cooper–Cohen connection through FOIA.

The evidence presently supports that **a Clinton-era attempt to investigate Cooper's Edwards claim was made**, based on Mellon's own later testimony. It does not yet show that Cohen found the film, that the film was classified in a black program, or that Cooper's later landing description is accurate.
