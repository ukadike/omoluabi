## TRACK A — CKY / Charlie Red Star film, Carman, Manitoba, 13 May 1975

### Jesse/Cameron timestamp

Approx. **00:26:20–00:28:10**.

Cameron says a CKY television team filmed the Carman object in May 1975 and that the film later appeared in *UFOs: It Has Begun*. He says the person who operated the camera was ordinarily a film editor/lab worker, not a regular cameraman, and had volunteered after staff declined unpaid night work.

### What we can independently anchor

**Library and Archives Canada** has a National Research Council record for:

- Location: **Carman, Manitoba**
- Sighting date: **13 May 1975**
- Document date: **14 May 1975**
- Record number: **4688-6653-11796**
- Record group: **National Research Council**
- Series/file context: Herzberg Institute of Astrophysics reports on non-meteoric sightings/UFOs

Archived LAC result:
https://www.collectionscanada.gc.ca/databases/ufo/001057-110.01-e.php?q3=Carman

Parent archival file:
- RG77
- Volume 308
- Microfilm **T-1742**
- File **UAR/N75/001-062**
- Date range 30 Dec 1974–30 May 1975
- LAC item ID **1765070**

Current LAC record:
https://recherche-collection-search.bac-lac.gc.ca/eng/Home/Record?IdNumber=1765070&app=fonandcol

This means the exact night associated with the film has an official Canadian federal record trail.

### Surviving footage trail

A copy of the CKY sequence survives in the documentary **UFOs: It Has Begun**. A public copy is available online; the documentary narration identifies the Carman sequence as 13 May 1975 and describes two separate sightings.

Cameron's archived research says the original CKY broadcast credited **Martin Rugne**, who ran CKY's processing lab and was not normally a cameraman. It says this was his first camera assignment.

Grant Cameron archive source:
https://files.bluebookfiles.org/pdfs/18266.pdf

### Name conflict to resolve

There are at least three names in circulation for the camera operator:

- documentary narration / transcript variants: **Alan Carr**

This is not a trivial spelling issue. The original CKY May 14 broadcast credit, personnel files, production log, or film leader should resolve it.

### Film-custody breakthrough

A critical review preserved in Cameron's archive states that private copies existed:

- one correspondent says they had copies **on film and videotape**;
- says **Grant's copy was cleaner**, with fewer scratches;
- says CKY had borrowed the correspondent's copy to make a new videotape because **CKY's own copy was lost**;
- says Ed Barker had no copy but thought **Bill Kendricks might**.

Source:
https://files.bluebookfiles.org/pdfs/18608.pdf

This means the CKY evidence is not simply “lost.” At least several derivative copies existed in private custody.

That critique must be tested against the **cleanest surviving film-generation copy**, not a repeatedly transferred VHS/video derivative.

## TRACK C — Vandenberg missile film

### Jesse/Cameron timestamp

Approx. **01:10:56–01:14:30**.

Cameron says Robert Emenegger told him that a film of a Vandenberg missile launch followed by unusual objects was not returned with other government footage and was likely still held by Emenegger's production partner **Allan Sandler**.

Cameron says:
- Emenegger checked with Sandler and said Sandler still had it;
- Sandler's vault was in Oregon;
- journalist Angelia Joiner was offered an opportunity to view it;
- filmmaker James Fox later contacted Sandler;
- Cameron recounts a proposed **$25,000** licensing/use fee that was later withdrawn after legal advice;
- Cameron says Fox subsequently raised the alleged classified film with congressional contacts.

This account is repeated in Cameron's March 2026 *DEBRIEFED* interview, which gives a second public timestamped telling of the same chain.

### Independent pieces that are verifiable

- Robert Emenegger and Allan Sandler were real production partners.
- They produced *UFOs: Past, Present, and Future* / *UFOs: It Has Begun*.
- U.S. copyright records identify **Sandler Institutional Films** and Emenegger/Sandler productions.
- James Fox has separately told a similar story about contacting Emenegger and being directed to Sandler.

### Missing decisive evidence

- actual can/reel;
- frame scan;
- label showing classification;
- documented chain from Air Force/Norton to Emenegger/Sandler;
- congressional letter/subpoena;
- Sandler acknowledgment in a primary written record;
- missile launch ID and camera station numbers.

### Retrieval targets

1. Allan Sandler / Sandler Institutional Films estate or corporate archive.
2. Oregon storage/vault inventory.
3. Robert Emenegger production files.
4. James Fox emails/notes concerning Sandler and the $25,000 discussion.
5. Angelia Joiner correspondence with Emenegger/Sandler.
