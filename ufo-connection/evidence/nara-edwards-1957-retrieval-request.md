# NARA Retrieval Request Packet — Edwards AFB, May 1957

**Purpose:** obtain the highest-generation records needed to reproduce the Project Blue Book balloon conclusion and test later witness/Cooper claims.

## Records requested

### 1. Audiovisual item 1003460

Project Blue Book audiovisual transfer inventory entry:

- **Item / case number:** 1003460
- **Date:** May 2, 1957
- **Location:** Edwards AFB, California
- **Observer/source:** Gettys & Bittick
- **Description:** two 35mm negative black-and-white filmstrips

Requested deliverables:
- full-resolution scans of both filmstrips, uncropped;
- film edges, frame numbers, fiducials and sprocket areas included;
- any can/envelope labels, accession sleeves or accompanying documentation;
- preservation/digitization metadata;
- known generation (original negative, duplicate negative, copy film, etc.);
- current archival series/box/container/reel location.

### 3. Identifier reconciliation

Please clarify whether the same Edwards photographic material is associated with:
- **Project Blue Book 4715**, and/or
- **Case 4750 Photos, USA**

A public derivative labeled 4715 says “41 photos.” A separate public-domain derivative is labeled “Case 4750 Photos, USA,” also for Edwards AFB on May 2, 1957. We are seeking the archival relationship between these identifiers and the photograph-roll boundary.

## Reproduction requirements

For visual forensics, compressed thumbnails are insufficient. Please preserve:
- original pixel dimensions available from the scan;
- full frame and edge information;
- no automatic crop;
- no contrast “enhancement” without an unaltered version;
- page/frame order;
- file names or archival sequence numbers.

## Accessibility/public-interest intent

The resulting Omoluabi public evidence dossier will provide:
- source-of-record attribution;
- descriptive alt text;
- transcripts for audio;
- provenance and derivative disclosures;
- machine-readable evidence metadata;
- explicit separation of official conclusions, witness testimony and unresolved claims.

No request is being made for classified or restricted material; this request concerns declassified/public Project Blue Book holdings.
