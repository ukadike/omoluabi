EDITORIAL METHOD

This is not a sightings database.

Omoluabi selects and reads each case before publication. The audience does not search, rank, vote on, or submit records here. Each page holds the official release beside a human-governed account of what it supports, what it cannot establish, and what a fuller multimodal record would require.

THE OMOLUABI READING

Every case holds five distinct layers.

  1. Official recordWhat the responsible public institution released.
  2. Observed signalWhat the named sensor or witness account actually registers.
  3. ContextEnvironmental, spatial, technical, and historical conditions that could change a reading.
  4. MissingnessData that are absent, inaccessible, withheld, or not described in the public release.
  5. Protected uncertaintyWhat must remain unresolved rather than being converted into a spectacle.

Governing principle: The device captures space. The controller orders time. The human decides what can responsibly become public knowledge.
