## Accessible summary

While Apollo 17 is traveling toward the Moon, the crew reports small bright lights outside the spacecraft. They describe the visible material as particles or fragments with jagged or angular appearances, drifting near the Apollo spacecraft and the separated Saturn S-IVB stage. During the same exchange the crew considers paint chips or ice chips as likely explanations and notes that some of the material appears to twinkle and move away from the S-IVB.

## Evidence reading
