## Accessible summary

Approximately one hour and forty-one minutes into Mercury-Atlas 9, Cooper reports seeing the phenomenon Mercury astronauts had called “fireflies.” The released AARO/DVIDS description identifies the visible particles as frozen condensation separating from the exterior of the spacecraft. Sunlight reflecting from the particles accounts for their bright white and greenish appearance in the official description.
