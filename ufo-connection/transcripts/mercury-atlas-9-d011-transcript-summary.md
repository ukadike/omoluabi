## Accessible summary

During Mercury-Atlas 9, Cooper describes the blue appearance of sunrise below the atmospheric haze layer. As he approaches sunrise he reports small, brilliant white luminous particles drifting away from Faith 7. The source description also places a later “fireflies” observation after Cooper deployed spherical mission equipment fitted with xenon strobe lights.

## Evidence reading

The record is useful because it preserves both the sensory observation and nearby spacecraft/mission context. A luminous point seen near a spacecraft cannot be interpreted responsibly without accounting for illumination, spacecraft shedding, condensation, deployed equipment and relative motion.

## Media description

Audio-only spacecraft communications. Cooper verbally describes sunrise, luminous particles and mission-related equipment; the released item does not supply synchronized external video of those particles.
